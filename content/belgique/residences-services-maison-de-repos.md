# Moving between a serviced residence and the nursing home

:::{rh-description}
Move a resident from a unit of the serviced residence (RS) to a bed of the MR/MRS, or back, through the internal transfer: the file, the Katz and the family come along, without retyping.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
How do I move a resident of the RS to the MR?
: On their contract, in the **Occupants** tab, click **Move to the rest home** on their line. The internal transfer window opens, as for an MR ↔ MRS transfer.

Is the unit released automatically?
: No. The contract goes on, marked **To release**, and the unit is billed until you release it on the day it is handed back.

What is sent to the mutuality?
: From the RS to the MR/MRS: a new admission, with Annexe 7 and the Katz, and no Annexe 11, since the mutuality knows nothing of the unit. From the MR/MRS to the RS: Annexe 11 for the departure, and nothing else.

Why do I not see the Move to the rest home button?
: The transfer reads the Katz: the button only shows to the users who may read Katz assessments.

Can I undo the transfer?
: Yes, by cancelling the new stay, as for any internal transfer, as long as the unit has not been released. Once the unit is handed back, the transfer can no longer be undone.
:::

When a resident of the residence needs a bed, or a nursing home resident is
ready to live independently again, the move goes through the **internal
transfer** the nursing home already uses: the record, the Katz, the family and
the documents stay with the person. See
[Internal transfer (MR ↔ MRS)](ehealth/eagreement-transfert.md) for the window
itself.

## From the residence to the nursing home

1. Open the resident's contract (**Serviced Living → Contracts**).
2. In the **Occupants** tab, click **Move to the rest home** on their line.
3. In the **Internal Transfer** window, choose the **Destination type** (MR or
   MRS), the **New room** — a bed, never a unit — the **Transfer date** and the
   **Transfer time**.
4. If the Katz has changed, tick **With Katz category change** and choose or
   record the new assessment.
5. Click **Confirm transfer**.

The window reminds what happens: *From a unit to the rest home: a new
admission, Annexe 7 with the Katz, no Annexe 11. The contract goes on until the
unit is released.*

The resident's stay in the unit ends and their MR or MRS stay opens. On the
contract, their line gets a **Left on** date; when they were the last occupant,
the contract shows **To release**.

:::{admonition} Release the unit afterwards
:class: warning

The unit stays billed until it is released. Once the flat is emptied and the
keys are back, click **Release the unit** on the contract. Depending on the
regime, the month started may still be due in full. See
[Notice and release of a unit](../residences-services/preavis-liberation.md).
:::

## From the nursing home to the residence

1. Write a contract on the unit, with the resident as an occupant, and **Sign**
   it — see [Contracts and occupants](../residences-services/contrats.md). The
   unit needs a signed contract.
2. On the resident's MR or MRS stay, click **Internal Transfer**.
3. Choose **RS** as the **Destination type**, the unit as the **New room**, the
   **Transfer date** and the **Transfer time**.
4. Click **Confirm transfer**.

The window reminds what happens: *From the rest home to a unit: Annexe 11 for the
departure, and nothing else goes to the insurer. The unit needs a signed
contract.* No Katz category change is asked. The contract becomes **Running**
with the move-in.

## Undoing a transfer

Cancelling the new stay reopens the previous one, and puts the resident back
as a present occupant of their contract. When the unit has been released in the
meantime, the cancellation is refused: the flat was handed back.
