# Medication follow-up

:::{rh-description}
What the Follow-up tab of the Medicines workstation brings to light in Resthome: residents back from hospital whose treatments to reconcile, treatments refused several times in a row, and residents whose medication deserves a review.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Why must the treatments be reconciled after a hospital stay?
: The return resumes the treatments exactly as they were before the departure. The hospital may have stopped, changed or added some: a nurse compares them with the discharge letter and keeps, stops or changes each one.

When is a refusal "repeated"?
: When a resident refused the same treatment three times in a row. The prescriber is told of each refusal; from the third in a row, the house is asked once to review the treatment.

What makes a resident appear under Medication Review?
: Ten running treatments or more, a psychotropic running for more than 90 days, a return from hospital not reconciled yet, or an allergy recorded against a running treatment.

Who sees these lists?
: Nurses and physicians see the returns from hospital and the medication review; the repeated refusals of their sectors show to every medication user.
:::

The **Follow-up** tab of the [Medicines workstation](index.md) gathers what did
not go as planned and calls for a decision. Each list also has its smart button
at the top of the workstation: **Back From Hospital**, **Repeated Refusals**,
**To Review**.

## Back from hospital: reconcile the treatments

When a resident returns from hospital, the treatments the departure had
suspended are resumed as they were. The resident's record then shows a banner,
*Back from hospital on …*, with **Reconcile the treatments**, and the
workstation lists the resident under **Back From Hospital**, with the date and
the number of running treatments. The head nurses on duty also receive an
activity.

1. Click **Reconcile** (on the workstation) or **Reconcile the treatments** (on
   the resident's record).
2. The window lists every running treatment: medicine, dose, frequency, moments
   and state. Untick **Keep** for each treatment the hospital stopped: it ends
   today.
3. Write in the note what the discharge letter says about the treatments.
4. Click **Reconciled**. A summary is posted on the resident, the banner and
   the activity close.

A treatment the hospital changed or added is then written as a new treatment
or from its prescription. See [Treatments](traitements.md).

## Repeated refusals

Each refusal recorded on the round is written on the treatment, for the
prescriber, with the reason and who recorded it. When a resident refuses the
same treatment **three times in a row**, one alert asks for a review of the
treatment (*Refused 3 times in a row*). The workstation lists these treatments
under **Repeated Refusals**, with their open activity.

## Medication review

A resident appears under **Medication Review** when one of these signs is
there:

- **ten running treatments or more**;
- a **psychotropic** (antidepressant, anxiolytic, antipsychotic, hypnotic)
  running for more than **90 days**;
- a return from hospital whose treatments are **not reconciled**;
- an **allergy** recorded against a running treatment.

The list shows the running treatments, the long-running psychotropics and the
reason; **The Treatments** opens them. The reason is also shown on the agenda
of the multidisciplinary meeting.
