# Simulations and live tracking

:::{rh-description}
Trying a change on the institutional allowance in Resthome with a simulation, and following the year in progress with live tracking.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
How do I test the effect of a hire?
: Click **Simulate a change**: the calculation is duplicated, you change the figures in the copy, and the result is compared with the reference.

Can a live tracking be validated?
: No. It follows a period that is still open, so its figure is not final.

Where do I see the allowance over several years?
: In the Forfait dashboard of the reporting application: allowance per year, breakdown by part, staff and dependency mix.
:::

## Simulating a change

**Simulate a change** duplicates the calculation as a variant. Name what
changes, adjust the figures — a hire, a different dependency mix — and compute.

The variant then shows the **allowance** and, next to it, the difference
**against the reference**. The reference calculation itself is untouched, and
the variants are listed under the **Simulations** button.

:::{admonition} A variant starts without results
:class: note

Duplicating clears the copied results. Otherwise the variant would show the
parent's allowance with a difference of 0.00 € before anything was computed.
:::

## Following the year in progress

A calculation marked **Live tracking** follows the period that is still open: it
is recomputed every time the menu is opened, so you see where the facility
stands without waiting for the period to close.

- It is **excluded** from the reference calculations and from the dashboard, so
  it never passes for a settled figure.
- It **cannot be validated**.
- A nightly refresh exists, delivered switched off.

## The dashboard

The **Forfait** dashboard of the reporting application reads the validated
calculations: allowance per funding year, breakdown by part, staff, and
dependency mix.
