# New task

:::{rh-description}
Writing a task on the spot in the WideCare app: a care act nobody planned, an unplanned medication, or work for the rest of the staff.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Where do I add a task?
: With the **+** button of the **Tasks** screen or of the **Round**, or with **New task** on a resident's sheet.

Can I record a care I have just given without planning it first?
: Yes. Choose **Care act**, name it and leave **Already done now** ticked: it is recorded as done, now, by you.

Who may record an unplanned medication?
: A nurse or a physician. The choice does not appear for the other roles.

Can I ask the technical team to change a bulb?
: Yes. Choose **Other task**, write what is to be done, choose the kind and the team, and when it is due.
:::

A task written on the spot covers what no plan foresaw. Open it:

- with the **+** button of the [Tasks](soins.md) screen;
- with the **+** button of the [Round](tour-medicaments.md) — it opens on the
  unplanned medication for whoever may give one;
- with **New task** on a [resident's sheet](habitants.md) — the resident is
  already filled in.

The kinds of task offered depend on your account. When only one is offered, the
choice does not show.

## Care act

A care act nobody planned, for a resident.

1. Choose **Care act**.
2. Under **Resident**, search by name or room.
3. Under **What was done**, search the care acts of the catalogue, or write it
   in your words. A care written in your words also asks for its **Type of
   care**.
4. Choose a **Moment (optional)**.
5. Add a word if useful.
6. Leave **Already done now** ticked to record it as done now. Untick it to
   create a care to do; **Assign it to me** then puts it on your list.
7. Tap **Record as done** or **Create the task**.

## Other task

Work for the rest of the staff: a room to tidy, a bulb to change, a step of a
move-in.

1. Choose **Other task**.
2. Name the **Resident (optional)** when the task is about somebody.
3. Under **What**, write what is to be done.
4. Choose the **Kind**: housekeeping, technical, administrative, move-in,
   reception, kitchen or other.
5. Choose the **Team** — any team of the house, or **Usual team**.
6. Choose **By**: **Today** or **Tomorrow**.
7. Tick **Already done now** if it is done, or **Assign it to me**.
8. Tap **Create the task** (or **Record as done**).

The task appears in the **Tasks** list of the team concerned, and in the back
office.

## Unplanned medication

A medication that is **not** on the resident's treatment sheet: a one-off order.
Only a nurse or a physician records one.

:::{warning}
A dose of a planned treatment is given from the [round](tour-medicaments.md),
never from here.
:::

1. Choose **Unplanned medication**.
2. Under **Resident**, search by name or room.
3. Under **Medication**, search the medication by name. The search needs the
   network.
4. Enter the **Quantity**.
5. Under **Why it was given**, write the reason: pain, fever, the physician's
   order…
6. Under **Ordered by (optional)**, name the physician who ordered it.
7. Tap **Record as given**.

The dose is recorded as given, by you, now, in the resident's medication
history.
