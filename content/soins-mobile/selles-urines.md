# Stools and urine

:::{rh-description}
Recording stools and urine from the WideCare app: passed or not, the Bristol type, the volume of urine, and the follow-up that flags a resident to watch.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Must I record when nothing was passed?
: Yes, when you checked. Tap **Nothing passed**: the observation is kept, and the delay since the last one keeps counting.

When is a resident flagged?
: For stools, from 60 hours without any (to watch) and past 72 hours (alert). For urine, from 20 hours (to watch) and past 24 hours (alert).

Is the alert a diagnosis?
: No. It is a reminder to look. A resident with no observation at all reads **Not recorded**, never "fine".

Does saving tick the care?
: Yes, when the screen was opened from a stools or urine care: the button then reads **Save and tick the care**.
:::

The **Stools / urine** button of the resident's sheet opens the screen. A
stools or urine care of the [Tasks](soins.md) list opens it too, for that kind.

## Recording an observation

1. Choose **Stools** or **Urine**. The line below says where the follow-up
   stands: how long since the last one, and the volume of urine over 24 hours.
2. Under **Passed?**, tap **Yes** or **Nothing passed**.
3. For stools, choose the **Bristol type**, from type 1 (separate hard lumps) to
   type 7 (liquid).
4. For urine, enter the **Volume (mL), if measured**.
5. Add a remark under **Anything to add**: pad change, catheter bag, aspect…
6. Tap **Save** (or **Save and tick the care**).

## The follow-up

Each resident followed carries a status for stools and for urine:

- **Not recorded** — no observation at all;
- **Up to date**;
- **To watch** — stools: 60 hours without any; urine: 20 hours;
- **Alert** — stools: past 72 hours; urine: past 24 hours.

The delay counts from the last stools or urine documented. Recording **Nothing
passed** does not reset it.

The status shows:

- on the resident's sheet, in the **stools and urine** card;
- in the bell, under **Stools and urine**: the residents to watch or in alert,
  the alerts first. A line opens the resident's sheet.

:::{note}
The volume over 24 hours adds up the volumes recorded. It is only as complete as
the recording, and is not a fluid balance.
:::
