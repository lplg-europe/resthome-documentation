# Rooms

:::{rh-description}
The rooms to prepare and to clean in the WideCare app: the housekeeping team starts a room and marks it ready with one swipe, and the housekeeping board follows.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Where does the list of rooms come from?
: From the housekeeping board of the back office: a room goes to "to prepare" before an arrival and to "to clean" after a departure.

How do I mark a room ready?
: Swipe the room to the right: **Start** when you begin, then **Ready** when it is done.

Why does a room say "Handled at the desk"?
: It was put on the board by hand at the desk, with no task behind it: the desk moves it on.

Can I start a room without Wi-Fi?
: No. A room is started and marked ready live, on the board's own state: tap again once the network is back.
:::

The **Rooms** screen gives the housekeeping team the rooms to get ready. Open
it with the **To prepare** or **To clean** card of the
[shift screen](service.md): the first says the hour of the next arrival, the
second how many rooms are being done.

## The list of rooms

The buttons at the top narrow the list: **All**, **To prepare**, **To clean**.
The rooms are grouped:

- **In progress** — the rooms being done, first: that is where you stand. The
  line says who started it and when;
- **To prepare** — before an arrival: the line says whose arrival and when;
- **To clean** — after a departure or a room change.

Each room shows its sector, the residents concerned, and **Late** when the
deadline has passed.

### Precautions

When a room calls for precautions — an isolation recorded in the care
registers — they are said in a **Precautions** block above the rooms, and on the
room's line, in the register's own words. Read them before the cart leaves.

## Starting and finishing a room

1. Swipe the room to the right: **Start**. The room moves to **In progress**.
2. Once done, swipe it to the right again: **Ready**. The room leaves the list.

A message offers **Undo** for a few seconds. The housekeeping board of the back
office follows each step, and the other way round: a room the desk moves to
ready leaves the list.

When every room is done, the screen says **Every room is ready**.

:::{note}
A room is started and marked ready with the connection on: the stage of the room
at the tap is what counts. Without a connection, the app says so; tap again once
the network is back.
:::
