# Meals

:::{rh-description}
The meal round in the WideCare app: the couverts of the meal resident by resident, served, refused, later or absent, the amount eaten after the meal, and the resident's meal sheet.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Why is the meal list empty?
: The couverts are laid when the kitchen confirms its menu. Until then, the screen says the kitchen has not confirmed the meal yet.

How do I serve everyone at the table at once?
: Tap **Serve all**. A sheet confirms; the absent, the residents away, the plates put off to later and the plates with a critical dietary warning are left out.

Why can a plate not be swiped "Served"?
: The plate carries a critical dietary warning, in red: change the dish first with **Change dish**.

Must I note the amount eaten?
: For the residents followed for nutrition, yes: their line shows **To note** after the meal. For the others it is optional.
:::

The **Meals** screen is the meal round on the phone. Whoever serves the meal
opens it with the meal card of the [shift screen](service.md), or from a
resident's sheet. It shows the couverts the kitchen laid when it confirmed its
menu: the phone lays nothing.

## Choosing the meal and the place

- The **meal** buttons at the top — breakfast, lunch, dinner… with their hour.
  The dishes of the menu show under them, with the alternatives.
- **Dining room**, **Room** or **All** — where the couverts are served.

A line counts the couverts **to serve**, **served** and **absent**, and, after
the meal, the amounts still to note.

When the kitchen has not confirmed the meal, or has cancelled it, the screen
says so.

## What a couvert shows

The couverts are in three lists: **To serve**, **Served** and **Absent**. Each
line shows the resident, and what to know at the table:

- the **texture**, highlighted when it is modified;
- the **diets**, the help needed at the table, the medical devices, the food
  allergies;
- the **Nutrition cares** of the period, with who is assigned — meal assistance,
  intake monitoring — ticked when done;
- what the resident does not like;
- the dietary warning — in **red** when it is critical;
- the resident's own dishes, when they differ from the menu.

## Serving

- **Swipe right** — **Served**.
- **Swipe left** — **Refused**: choose why, add a word, tap **Refused**.
- **Press and hold** — every choice:
  - **In room** or **Dining room** — moves the couvert;
  - **Later** — stays to serve, not late: fasting for an exam, not up yet;
  - **Absent** — absent from the table;
  - **Change dish** — the dishes of the menu with no warning for this resident;
  - **Note the amount** — see below;
  - **Undo** — takes the last act back.

A plate with a critical dietary warning cannot be served as it is: change the
dish first.

**Serve all** marks every couvert still to serve as served, after a
confirmation. The absent, the residents away, the plates put off to later and
the plates with a critical dietary warning are left out.

## Noting the amount eaten

After the meal, tap **Note the amount** on a served couvert.

1. Under **Amount eaten**, tap 0, ¼, ½, ¾ or 1.
2. Under **A drink with it**, choose the kind of drink and the amount (100, 150,
   200 or 250 ml).
3. Add a word if useful.
4. Tap **Save**.

For a resident followed for nutrition, the button is highlighted and the line
shows **To note** until the amount is noted. Noting it closes the resident's
**intake monitoring** care of the period: the sheet says which care it closes.

**Refused? give the reason** switches to the refusal.

## The resident's meal sheet

The **Meals** button of a resident's sheet opens what the resident ate:

- **Today** — the day's plates, with their outcome, the hour, who served them
  and the amount eaten; **Open the meal round** goes to the round;
- **Intake of the day** — energy, protein and fluids against the targets, the
  coverage of the last three days, and **Today's drinks**;
- **At the table** — texture, diets, food allergies, help needed, what the
  resident likes and does not like.

A banner says when the resident is below the targets these last days, or is
followed for nutrition: tell the nurse.

See also [The meal distribution round](../repas/distribution.md) and
[Nutritional monitoring](../repas/suivi-nutritionnel.md) in the back office.
