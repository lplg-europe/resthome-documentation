# Pillboxes

:::{rh-description}
Preparing and checking the pillboxes in the WideCare app: day by day, resident by resident, prepared by the house or received from the pharmacy, then checked by another nurse.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Who sees the pillboxes?
: The nurses. The pillboxes button sits at the top of the **Round** screen.

How far ahead can a pillbox be prepared?
: Seven days ahead at most: the screen offers today and the next days of the pillbox's week.

Can I check a box I prepared myself?
: No. A box is checked by another nurse. When you prepared the whole box, the screen says so instead of offering **Conform**.

What happens when a medicine is missing from the box?
: Tap it on **Prepare**: it is marked missing and the pharmacy is told.

What does tracking the pillboxes change?
: With **Track the pillboxes** on, each dose records who prepared it and who checked it, a care assistant only gives a prepared dose, and a nurse prepares an unprepared one on the spot.
:::

The **Pillboxes** screen follows the preparation of the medication ahead of the
rounds: who prepared each box, who checked it, and what is missing. Open it with
the **pillboxes** button at the top of the [Round](tour-medicaments.md). It is
shown to nurses.

## Setting it up

*Settings > Medical*, **Track the pillboxes**. With the setting on:

- each dose records who prepared it and who checked it;
- a care assistant gives only a prepared dose;
- a nurse prepares an unprepared dose on the spot, at the round.

Without the setting, the screen still works, and says that a care assistant may
still give an unprepared dose.

## Choosing the day

The days at the top go from **Today** to seven days ahead. Choose the day of the
boxes to prepare or to check.

Each resident shows the box moment by moment (morning, noon, evening…), with the
medicines and their dose, and the state of the box: **To prepare**, **In
progress**, **Prepared**, **Prepared, some missing**, **Checked** or **Issue to
fix**, with who prepared or checked it.

## Preparing the boxes

On the **Prepare** tab, choose who prepared the doses under **Prepared by**:

- **the house** — the nurses fill the boxes;
- **the pharmacy** — the pharmacy delivers sachets.

### Prepared by the house

1. Fill the box.
2. Tap a medicine that is missing: it is marked **missing** and the pharmacy is
   told. Tap it again once found.
3. Tap **Pillbox prepared**.

Syrups and drops are not in the box: they show **at the moment** and are
prepared when they are given.

### Received from the pharmacy

The sachets hold what the pharmacy may put in them; a medicine marked **house**
stays yours to prepare.

1. Tap **Sachets received and stored** under the resident.
2. Fill in **Delivered on**, **Sachets until**, the **Lot or series, printed on
   the sachet** and the **Number of sachets**, and a remark if useful.
3. Tap **Sachets received and stored**.

The resident's box then shows the delivery: its date, its lot, and the day the
sachets must be used by. A sachet past its use-by date is shown, to prepare
again.

## Checking the boxes

On the **Check** tab:

- tap **Conform** under a resident to check the whole box;
- tap a medicine to flag an issue on that dose: **Wrong medicine**, **Wrong
  dose**, **Wrong moment**, **Dose missing** or **Dose in excess**, with a remark
  if useful, then **Record the issue**.

A dose with an issue is blocked until a nurse prepares it again: on **Prepare**,
it shows **issue · prepare again**; tap it once fixed. At the round, it shows
**Issue at the check**.

:::{note}
The doses you prepared yourself are left out of your check: another nurse
checks them.
:::

## Taking a medicine out of a box

When a treatment stops or changes after the box was prepared, the resident shows
**To take out of the box**, with the medicines concerned. Tap each one once it is
removed.
