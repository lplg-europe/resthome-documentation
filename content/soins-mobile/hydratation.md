# Hydration

:::{rh-description}
Recording drinks in the WideCare app: one tap per glass, the day's total against the target, the residents with no drink yet, and the hydration rounds ticked on their own.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
How do I record a glass of water?
: On **Hydration**, tap the amount under the resident: 100, 150, 200 or 250 ml. One tap is one glass.

How do I record another drink than water?
: Tap the kind of drink beside the amounts, or press and hold an amount: choose the kind, the amount, and tap **Record**.

Can I correct a glass recorded by mistake?
: Yes. On the resident's drinks, swipe the line to the left to **Delete** it, or press and hold it to **Edit** the amount, the kind or the hour.

Can I record a glass without Wi-Fi?
: Yes. The glass is kept on the phone and filed at the hour it was given when the network comes back.
:::

The **Hydration** screen follows the day's drinks of the residents of the
sector. Open it with the **Hydration** card of the [shift screen](service.md),
with the **Drink** button of a resident's sheet, or from a **hydration round**
care of the [Tasks](soins.md) list.

## The residents' drinks

Each resident shows:

- what was drunk today against the target, in ml, and a bar;
- the hour of the last drink, or **No drink recorded today**;
- **Below target these last days** when the resident has drunk too little.

The buttons at the top narrow the list: **All**, **No drink yet**, **Below
target**.

## Recording a drink

- **Tap an amount** — 100, 150, 200 or 250 ml: one glass of the kind shown.
- **Tap the kind of drink** — or **press and hold an amount** — to choose
  another drink or another amount: pick the **Kind of drink**, the **Amount
  (ml)**, add a word if useful, then tap **Record**.

Each glass is dated at the moment of the tap. It ticks the **hydration round**
care of its period.

## One resident's drinks

From a resident's sheet, or a hydration round care, the screen shows that
resident only, with **Today's drinks**: each glass, its hour, its amount, its
kind and who recorded it. **Every resident** goes back to the whole sector.

- **Swipe left** — **Delete** the glass.
- **Press and hold** — **Edit** the glass: the kind, the amount, the hour it
  was drunk (step it back or forward, or tap the clock), and a word.

You may take back the glasses you recorded; whoever may delete lines of the
fluid chart may take back anybody's. Deleting or moving a glass gives back the hydration round it had
ticked, unless another glass of the period covers it.

See also [Nutritional monitoring](../repas/suivi-nutritionnel.md) in the back
office.
