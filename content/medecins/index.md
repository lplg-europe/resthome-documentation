# General practice

:::{rh-description}
WideCare for a general practitioner in their own practice: the Practice app, the patients, the appointments, the consultations with their SOAP note and the acts they bill.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Where do I find my practice in WideCare?
: In the **Practice** app on the home screen. Its menus are **Patients**, **Appointments**, **Consultations** and **Billable Acts**; your country adds its own services below them.

Does a practice need rooms or stays?
: No. A practice has patients, not residents: no bed, no room, no stay. Each patient has one card, and everything done for them is filed on it.

What is the Consultations app on my home screen?
: It opens the same appointments and consultations as the **Practice** app. The **Practice** app gathers them with the patients and the billed acts: work from there.

Who can use the Practice app?
: Users with the **Consultations** access. Billing the acts also needs the **Per-Act Billing** access. See [Practice settings](reglages.md).
:::

WideCare serves the general practitioner **in their own practice**, alone or
with colleagues: the patients, the diary, the consultation note and the acts
billed for it, in one app.

<!-- screenshot to add: the Practice app open on the weekly appointments calendar -->

## The Practice app

| Menu | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Patients** | The patients of the practice, one card each. |
| **Appointments** | The diary: one calendar, one colour per practitioner. |
| **Consultations** | Every encounter with a patient and its SOAP note. |
| **Billable Acts** | The acts performed, to invoice or already invoiced. |

## Key principles

- **Patient**: a person the practice cares for. Their card holds the identity,
  the contacts and the history of what was done for them.
- **Appointment**: a time slot in the diary with a patient, a practitioner and
  a reason. It follows the visit: planned, confirmed, arrived, seen.
- **Consultation**: one encounter, at the practice or at home. It records the
  reason and the **SOAP note** (subjective, objective, assessment, plan).
- **Billable act**: one act performed for the patient during the consultation,
  with its fee and who pays what.
- **Invoice**: the acts become a customer invoice, one per patient.

## A consultation, from the diary to the invoice

1. Book the slot in **Practice → Appointments**.
2. When the patient arrives, click **Patient Arrived**, then
   **Start Consultation**.
3. Write the **SOAP Note** and add the acts on the **Acts** tab.
4. Click **Close Consultation**, then **Create Invoice**.

:::{admonition} In Belgium
:class: rh-country rh-country-be

The Belgian practice adds the eID card reading, the insurability check, the
eAttest, the global medical file (DMG), the INAMI nomenclature and the eFact per
act: see [General practice in Belgium](../belgique/medecins/index.md).
:::
