# Patients

:::{rh-description}
The patients of a general practice in WideCare: the list, creating a patient, the patient card and its contacts, and finding a patient's consultations.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Why can't I find a person in the Patient field of an appointment?
: The field only offers the patients of the practice and the people already seen in consultation. Create the person in **Practice → Patients** first, then pick them.

Is a patient a contact too?
: Yes. The patient card and the contact are the same person: **Contact**, next to the name, opens the contact record, with its address and its invoices.

Where are a patient's past consultations?
: In **Practice → Consultations**: search for the patient's name, or group by **Patient**. The contact record of the patient also shows a **Consultations** button.
:::

The **Patients** menu lists the people the practice cares for. Each patient has
**one card**: the consultations, the appointments and the billed acts all point
to it.

## The list of patients

**Practice → Patients** opens the patients as cards; switch to the list to see
them in rows. Type a name in the search bar to find a patient.

## Create a patient

1. Click **New** in **Practice → Patients**.
2. Type the patient's name.
3. Fill in the **Identity** tab: **Date of Birth**, gender, **Nationality**,
   phone and email.
4. Save.

The patient is now offered in the appointments, the consultations and the acts.

## The patient card

The card shows the name, the patient's reference and age. **Contact**, next to
the name, opens the contact record of the same person.

The **Identity** tab holds:

- the identity: **Date of Birth**, **Place of Birth**, **Nationality**, the ID
  card, the languages;
- the contact details: phone and email;
- the doctors: **Attending Physician** and **Deputy Physician**;
- the **Contact Persons**: relatives and their relationship to the patient;
- the **Key Contacts**: the **Emergency Contact Person**, the **Legal
  Representative** and the **Trusted Person**.

:::{admonition} In Belgium
:class: rh-country rh-country-be

The card also carries the NISS, the mutuality and the insurability answer, and
the eID card fills in the identity: see
[The patient's file in Belgium](../belgique/medecins/patients-belgique.md).
:::

## A patient's consultations

- In **Practice → Consultations**, type the patient's name in the search bar, or
  group by **Patient**.
- On the contact record of the patient, the **Consultations** button opens them
  all, and **New** there starts a consultation for that patient.
