# Glossary

:::{rh-description}
Glossary of WideCare terms valid in every country: billing period, care allowance, dependency category, supplement envelope, family portal access, liaison notebook, pocket money, technical intervention.
:::

The terms used in WideCare and in nursing-home billing, valid in every country,
explained simply. Each entry links, where useful, to the page that covers it in
detail.

:::{admonition} In Belgium
:class: rh-country rh-country-be

The Belgian terms and acronyms — INAMI, Katz, NISS, OA, MDA, eFact,
eAgreement, the annexes, AViQ, CPAS, the noon rule — are explained in
[Glossary — Belgium](belgique/glossaire.md).
:::

---

## Access (family portal)

The authorisation given to **one relative** for **one resident**, scope by scope
(liaison notebook, documents, meals, invoices, health). It is what opens the
family portal; being a contact person opens nothing. Its status is **Draft**,
**Active** or **Revoked**.
See: [Granting an access](portail-famille/acces.md)

## Anticipatory billing

A billing mode where a month's **accommodation** is invoiced **the previous month**,
while the **care allowance** and the **supplements** are invoiced in the month they are
provided.
See: [Billing overview](facturation/index.md)

## Billing period

The **billing month** in WideCare, which moves through four states: **Draft**,
**Generated** (calculated), **Invoiced** and **Closed**.
See: [Billing a month, step by step](facturation/facturer-un-mois.md)

## Care allowance

The **daily** amount paid by the resident's health insurer for care, in the countries
that have one. It depends on the resident's **dependency category** and is calculated
on their **days of presence**.
See: [Billing overview](facturation/index.md)

## Contact person

A relative or close person recorded in the resident's file, with their
relationship and phone number. A contact person sees nothing of the file until
they receive a family portal **access**.
See: [Family portal](portail-famille/index.md)

## Credit note

Document that **cancels or refunds** all or part of an invoice. WideCare prepares one
automatically, for example on a departure partway through a month that has already been
invoiced (accommodation billed in advance).
See: [Departure and death](facturation/depart-deces.md)

## Day-counting rule

The convention that decides whether a day of presence counts toward the care
allowance. It depends on the country, hence the importance of departure and return
dates and times.
See: [Absences and hospitalisations](facturation/absences.md)

## Dependency category

The level of dependency given by the **dependency assessment**. The scale depends on
the country; the category drives the care allowance where the country pays one.
See: [Managing a resident](residents/gerer-un-resident.md)

## Document workspace

The resident's folder in the **Documents** app, with its medical, administrative
and billing subfolders. Attachments added to the resident are filed there
automatically.
See: [Resident documents](documents/index.md)

## Family space

The place a resident's team and family share: the family portal accesses, the
shared documents, and the liaison notebook. One per resident, created the first
time it is needed.
See: [Notebook, documents and invoices](portail-famille/carnet-documents.md)

## Health insurer

The body that insures the resident for health care. Depending on the country, it pays
the care allowance and exchanges data electronically with the facility.
See: [Managing a resident](residents/gerer-un-resident.md)

## Liaison notebook

The discussion thread of the family space: what the team writes there as a
message reaches the relatives on the portal, and they can answer. Internal
notes stay with the team.
See: [Notebook, documents and invoices](portail-famille/carnet-documents.md)

## Medicines workstation

The single screen of the **Medicines** entry of the care application: the
prescriptions, treatments, pharmacy orders, pillboxes, the round and the
catalogue, each behind a tab or a tile.
See: [Medicines](medicaments/index.md)

## National identification number

The resident's personal identification number in their country, recorded on the
resident's file. It is required for the electronic exchanges with the health insurer,
where the country has them.
See: [Managing a resident](residents/gerer-un-resident.md)

## Pharmacy mandate

The resident's written permission for the home to **order and receive** their
medicines at their pharmacy. Without a mandate in force, nothing is ordered for
them.
See: [Pharmacy orders](medicaments/pharmacie.md)

## Pillbox

The box prepared for a resident, by moment of the day, from their treatments;
one nurse prepares it and another checks it before the round.
See: [Pillboxes and the pharmacy's sachets](medicaments/piluliers.md)

## Pocket money

Money a third party pays to the home and that the home **holds for a
resident**; the resident's purchases are drawn from it. WideCare shows the
balance on the resident's record.
See: [Pocket money](argent-de-poche/index.md)

## Regime

The set of rules a public authority sets for the serviced residences it
licenses. Each residence runs under one regime; contracts, billing and
registers read their rules from it.
See: [Regimes](residences-services/regimes.md)

## Serviced residence

A residence where independent people rent a flat (a **unit**) and buy services
around it. There is no bed and no care allowance: each unit has a contract,
billed every month.
See: [Serviced residences](residences-services/index.md)

## Supplement envelope

The **monthly grouping of a resident's supplements** (products and services outside the
allowance). Any change automatically updates their invoice.
See: [Supplements](facturation/supplements.md)

## Technical intervention

A repair or a job in a resident's room, recorded as a Helpdesk ticket linked to
the resident, the room and the stay, with its full cost. It can be billed to
the resident.
See: [Technical interventions](interventions/index.md)

## Third-party payer

Mechanism where the **health insurer pays its share directly** to the facility, the
resident settling only their own share.

---

## Unit

A flat of a serviced residence, rented under one contract to one or more
occupants.
See: [Residences and units](residences-services/residences-logements.md)
