# Shifts for the staff

:::{rh-description}
How care staff read their shifts in WideCare — in My shifts, in the schedule email and on a personal page for the phone — and take an open shift of their role.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Where do I see my shifts?
: In Planning → My shifts, as a calendar, cards or a list. Only confirmed shifts appear there; a draft is not shown until the planner confirms it.

Do I need to log in to read my schedule on my phone?
: No. The schedule email has a See my schedule button that opens your personal page without logging in. The link is personal: do not share it.

How do I take an open shift?
: In the Open shifts menu of Planning, click Take this shift, or tap Take on your personal page. The shift becomes yours at once.

Why can I not take an open shift?
: It may ask for a role you do not hold, overlap one of your shifts, leave you less rest than the establishment requires, have already started, or the establishment may not let the staff take open shifts.
:::

The staff see only the **confirmed** shifts: what the planner is still preparing
stays out of sight until it is published.

## My shifts

Open **Planning → My shifts**. Your published shifts appear as a **calendar**,
**cards** or a **list**. Open one to read its sector, hours, label and the
handover **Notes**.

A user sees the confirmed shifts of their **team**, so they can also read who
works with them in **Planning → Schedule**.

## The schedule email

When the planner sends the schedule, each person whose shifts changed receives
an email with their shifts of the period and a **See my schedule** button.

The button opens a **personal page**, made for the phone:

- **My shifts** — your shifts of the coming six weeks, with their notes;
- **Open shifts you can take** — the open shifts of your role, each with a
  **Take** button.

The page needs no login. It is personal: keep its link to yourself.

## Take an open shift

An **open shift** is a confirmed shift that still has nobody. When the setting
**Open shifts** is on, anyone holding its role can take it:

- in the **Open shifts** menu of **Planning**, click **Take this shift** on the card or in the
  list; or
- on your personal page, tap **Take**.

The shift is yours at once, and its history notes who took it. The list opens on
the **Upcoming** shifts: a shift that has started can no longer be taken.

WideCare refuses the shift when:

- it is already taken, not published yet or already started;
- it asks for a role you do not hold;
- you already have a shift at that time;
- it would leave you less rest between two working days than the establishment
  requires;
- the establishment does not let the staff take open shifts.

:::{note}
To take a shift from the back office, your user must be linked to your staff
record. Your scheduling preferences do not block a shift you choose yourself.
:::
