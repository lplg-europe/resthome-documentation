# WideCare documentation

:::{rh-description}
The WideCare documentation: one site for every care sector — nursing homes, day care centres, serviced residences, general practitioners — with a space for each country's rules.
:::

**WideCare** is the family of healthcare software published by LPLG on Odoo.
Each care sector has its own pages, built on a shared base: the person's file,
the medical record, medication and the WideCare app work the same way
everywhere. Choose your sector:

:::{rh-suites}
:::

## How the documentation is organised

- **WideCare** — the software, and this page.
- **A sector** — nursing homes, day care centres, serviced residences or
  general practitioners. The sector selector at the top of the page keeps only
  the pages of your sector.
- **A country** — the country selector shows the common pages and the rules of
  your country.

The header always shows where you are: **WideCare › sector › country**. Click
**WideCare** to come back to this page, or open the sector menu to change
sector.
