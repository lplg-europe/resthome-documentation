# WideCare documentation

:::{rh-description}
The WideCare documentation: one suite per care trade — nursing homes, hospitals, dentists and more — each with its own documentation and a space for each country's rules.
:::

**WideCare** is the family of healthcare software published by LPLG on Odoo.
Each care trade has its own **suite**, built on a shared base: the person's
file, the medical record, medication and the care app work the same way
everywhere. Choose your suite:

:::{rh-suites}
:::

## How the documentation is organised

- **WideCare** — the family of suites, and this page.
- **A suite** — one trade, with its own documentation written in the words of
  that trade. **Resthome** is the suite for nursing and care homes.
- **A country** — inside a suite, the country selector at the top of the page
  shows the common pages and the rules of your country.

The header always shows where you are: **WideCare › suite › country**. Click
**WideCare** to come back to this page, or open the suite menu to go straight
to another suite.
