# Evaluations to validate

:::{rh-description}
One list of every assessment still open in WideCare, whatever the scale: drafts and confirmed evaluations waiting for validation, by resident, scale and evaluator.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Which evaluations appear in the list?
: Every evaluation still open — in draft or confirmed but not yet validated — of every scale, for the residents of the house. Residents who have died or are archived are left out.

How do I validate an evaluation from the list?
: Click its line: the evaluation opens on its own form, where you finish, confirm or validate it.

Where else do I see it?
: On the care dashboard, tile **Evaluations to Validate** in the **Follow-up** block.
:::

Assessments are filled in on several scales — cognition, pressure sores,
nutrition, pain, balance, dependency. **Evaluations to Validate** gathers, in
one list, all those still waiting.

## Open the list

**Nursing Care → Care → Assessments → Evaluations to Validate**, or the tile
**Evaluations to Validate** on the [care dashboard](tableau-de-bord.md).

Each line shows the resident, the scale, the date, the evaluator and the
state.

<!-- screenshot to add: the Evaluations to Validate list grouped by scale -->

## Work through it

1. Filter with **Draft**, **Confirmed** or **My Evaluations**.
2. Group by **Scale** or by **Resident**.
3. Click a line: the evaluation opens, to be finished, confirmed or validated.

Once validated, an evaluation leaves the list. It is then cited on the
resident's care plan as evidence to consider. See [Care plans](plans-de-soins.md).

:::{admonition} In Belgium
:class: rh-country rh-country-be

The list also holds the Katz evaluations still open. See
[The Katz assessment](../belgique/katz.md).
:::
