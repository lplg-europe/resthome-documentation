# Stools and urine

:::{rh-description}
The stools and urine follow-up in WideCare: record each passage or its absence, the Bristol type and the volume, and see at a glance which residents are to watch or in alert.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
When does a resident go "To watch" or "Alert"?
: For stools: to watch from 60 hours without stools, alert past 72 hours. For urine: to watch from 20 hours without urine, alert past 24 hours. A resident without any observation shows **Not recorded**.

Can I record that nothing was passed?
: Yes. Untick **Emission observed**: the observation counts as a check, not as a passage.

Is the alert a diagnosis?
: No. The statuses are reminders to look; the clinical judgement stays with the team.
:::

The follow-up of stools and urine tells the team, resident by resident, how
long it has been since the last passage.

## The follow-up list

**Nursing Care → Care → Observations → Stools and Urine** lists the residents
with, for stools and for urine:

- the status: **Not recorded**, **Up to date**, **To watch** or **Alert**;
- the last stools and their Bristol type, and the hours without stools;
- the last urine, the hours without urine and the volume over 24 hours.

Filters: **To watch or in alert**, **Stools in alert**, **Urine in alert**,
**Not recorded**.

| Follow-up | To watch | Alert |
|---|---|---|
| Stools | from 60 hours without stools | past 72 hours |
| Urine | from 20 hours without urine | past 24 hours |

## Record an observation

1. Click **Stools** or **Urine** on the resident's line — or **Record stools**
   / **Record urine** in the **Care** tab of the resident's form, or **Record
   the observation** on an elimination care task.
2. Check **Observed at**.
3. Leave **Emission observed** ticked when something was passed; untick it to
   note that nothing was.
4. For stools, pick the **Bristol type** (Type 1 to Type 7); for urine, the
   **Volume (mL)** if measured.
5. Add a **Note** if needed, and save.

**History** shows the resident's past observations.

## All observations

**Nursing Care → Care → Observations → Stool and Urine Observations** lists
every observation, with filters **Stools**, **Urine**, **Emission observed**,
**Nothing passed** and **Last 24 hours**, and a pivot view to count them.

:::{admonition} A reminder, not a diagnosis
:class: note

The statuses help the team notice what has not been seen for a while. They do
not replace the clinical judgement of the nurse or the physician.
:::
