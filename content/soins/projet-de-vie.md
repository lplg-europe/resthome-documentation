# The life project

:::{rh-description}
The resident's life project in Resthome: who they are and what they want — life story, habits, likes, interests, relationships, values and wishes — with a referent, a review date and objectives that land in the care plan.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Does every resident have a life project?
: Yes. Each resident in the house has one life project. The list under Nursing Care, Residents, Life Projects shows where each one stands.

What does the life project change in the care plan?
: Click **New Objective** on the life project: the objective is written for the resident and lands in their care plan, with the other care objectives.

Who can read the life project?
: Care staff. The life project is not shown to other users of Resthome.
:::

The **life project** says who the resident is and what they want, in their own
words. It complements the [anamnesis](anamnese.md), which records the facts
and the wishes for hospitalisation and the end of life.

## Find the life projects

- **Nursing Care → Residents → Life Projects** lists every resident's life
  project with its status, its referent and its number of objectives. Filters:
  **In the House**, **To Do**, **In Progress**, **Complete**, **My Life
  Projects**; group by **Status** or **Referent**.
- On the resident's form, the **Life Project** tab shows the status, the
  referent, the dates and what has been written, with **Start the Life
  Project** or **Open the Life Project**.

## 1. Start the life project

1. Open the resident's life project.
2. Set the **Referent** — the member of staff who follows it.
3. Pick the methods it is **Built With** — for example Potentia, Montessori,
   an interview with the resident or the family, daily observation.
4. Click **Start**: the status moves from **To Do** to **In Progress**.

## 2. Write what the team learns

One tab per theme, filled in over the weeks:

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Life Story** | Where they come from, their work, their family, the events that matter |
| **Habits and Rhythms** | Getting up, going to bed, meals, washing |
| **Likes and Dislikes** | Food, music, clothes, what pleases or upsets them |
| **Interests and Activities** | Hobbies, what they still like to do or would like to discover |
| **Relationships** | Family, friends, residents they get along with |
| **Values and Beliefs** | Convictions, spirituality, what must be respected |
| **Wishes and Projects** | What they want to keep doing, learn or achieve |

Set the **Next Review** date: when the project is to be read again with the
resident.

## 3. Turn wishes into objectives

Click **New Objective** (once the project is started). The objective is written
for the resident and goes into their [care plan](plans-de-soins.md), opened as a
draft if they have none. The **Objectives** tab and smart button list them,
with who they are assigned to, their target date, their observations and
whether they are achieved.

## 4. Complete or reopen

Click **Mark as Complete** when the project is written: the completion date is
recorded. **Reopen** brings it back to **In Progress**.

## Configure the methods

**Nursing Care → Configuration → Life Project Methods** (managers) lists the
methods offered in **Built With**.
