# The care dashboard

:::{rh-description}
The dashboard of the Nursing Care app in Resthome: what needs a hand now, today's agenda, the residents to keep an eye on, and one tile per care screen, for the whole house or one sector.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Can I see only my sector on the dashboard?
: Yes. Pick the sector in the **Sector** block; **The whole house** shows everything again. The choice applies to the counters, the lists and the tiles.

How do I record a fall or a hospital departure from the dashboard?
: Click **New** and pick what to record: a care task, an observation, vital signs, a multidisciplinary meeting, a fall, a wound, a restraint, a hospital departure or return, or an anamnesis.

Can I keep the screens I use most at the top?
: Yes. Click the star of a tile: it moves to **Favourites**, at the top of the dashboard.
:::

The **Dashboard** is the home page of the **Nursing Care** app
(**Nursing Care → Dashboard**). It is built for the start of a shift: what calls
for a hand now, what happens today, who to watch, and a door to every care
screen.

## Choose the sector

The **Sector** block lists the sectors of the house. Pick one to narrow the
whole dashboard to its residents, or **The whole house** to see everything.

## The work of the moment

- **To do now** — one line per thing that calls for a hand, with its button.
- **Waiting, nothing to do** — what someone else owes, shown in grey, without
  a button.

## The strip of the day

- **Presence** — who is in the house, who is away, who is in hospital, who is
  back today.
- **Today's care** — the care tasks of the day, done and missed, by sector.
- **Movements** — the admissions and departures of the day, and the recent
  arrivals.

Each figure opens the matching list.

## Keep an eye on, agenda, recently opened

- **Keep an eye on** — a short list of residents who need a second look, each
  with its reason (an observation requiring attention, for example).
- **Today's agenda** — the meetings, the care tasks at a set hour and the
  birthdays of the day.
- **Recently opened** — the residents you opened last.

## The tiles

The tiles are grouped in three blocks:

| Block | Tiles |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Medicines, Today's Care, My Assigned Care, To Assign, Observations, Vital Signs |
| **Follow-up** | Care Plans, Objectives, Record Reviews, Multidisciplinary Meetings, Evaluations to Validate |
| **Record** | Residents, Medical Record, Allergies, Anamneses, and the registers: Falls, Wounds, Restraints, Continence, Infections, Procedures, Isolations |

Click the star of a tile to pin it under **Favourites**.

## Record something new

Click **New** at the top of the dashboard and choose:

- **Care task**, **Observation**, **Vital signs**, **Multidisciplinary
  meeting**;
- **Fall**, **Wound**, **Restraint** — a new entry in the register;
- **Hospital departure or return** — see
  [Hospital departure and return](hospitalisation.md);
- **Anamnesis**.
