# Capability observation (Potentia)

:::{rh-description}
Observe what a resident can still do in everyday situations with the Potentia grid, turn each alternative into a care plan objective, and follow the observation in the multidisciplinary meeting.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
What is the capability observation for?
: It records what a resident can still do in everyday situations — finding their way, everyday gestures, reading and writing, understanding, social interactions — and, for each situation, the action or alternative that would let them do it anyway.

Is the capability observation an assessment of dependency?
: No. It observes preserved capacities; it does not replace the dependency assessment.

Where do I open a capability observation?
: In Nursing Care → Care → Observations → Capability Observations, from the + button of the care dashboard, from the multidisciplinary meeting, or on the care app with the Capacities button of the resident.

How does an observation reach the care plan?
: From a situation where an alternative is written, click Make it an objective or Add to the care plan. The synthesis then lists each alternative and what became of it.

Can several people fill the same observation?
: Yes. Each situation keeps its own observer and date; the observation lists every observer and is closed by the observer in charge.

When is the next observation due?
: Closing an observation proposes the next one a year later. The team can set an earlier date. Once that date has passed, the observation is to renew and comes up in the multidisciplinary meeting.
:::

The **capability observation** looks at what a person **still can do**, rather
than at what they can no longer do. It is filled situation by situation, by
several members of the team over days or weeks, then closed with a synthesis.
Its real purpose is the last column of the grid: the **actions or alternatives**
that become objectives of the care plan.

The application is **optional**: an establishment that does not install it sees
neither its menu nor its line in the multidisciplinary meeting.

:::{admonition} In Belgium
:class: rh-country rh-country-be

The grid delivered is **Potentia**, the observation grid proposed by the AViQ and
the CARD to Walloon nursing homes. It is a recommendation, not a duty, and it
does not replace the Katz scale. The observation opened on entry into an
adapted unit, and its place on the meeting's agenda after the first month,
follow the Walloon standards for adapted units (Annexe 120).
:::

## The grid

The grid delivered covers seven domains: spatial cues, temporal cues, everyday
gestures, reading, writing, understanding and social interactions. For each
situation the observer chooses how the person came through it:

- **Independent**;
- **With help**;
- **By the observer**.

What each level means is written **for each domain** ("finds the way alone",
"reads alone"…) and shown next to the choice. Some situations are only noted,
never graded; for some, the observer writes the question they asked.

Administrators read the grids in **Nursing Care → Configuration → Observation
Grids**: domains, situations, the meaning of the three levels and the category
an objective made from a domain gets. Add your own situations rather than
changing those of the grid.

## 1. Open an observation

Open **Nursing Care → Care → Observations → Capability Observations** and click
**New**, or use **Capability observation** on the **+** button of the care
dashboard, where a **Capacities (Potentia)** tile also sits in the follow-up.

Fill in **Who and why**:

- **Resident** and **Grid**;
- **Reason** — **Admission or entry into the unit**, **Periodic observation**,
  **After an event (a fall, a hospital stay, a change noticed)**, **Asked by the
  multidisciplinary meeting** or **Other**, with a **Reason detail**;
- under **When and by whom**, the **Opened on** date and the **Observer in
  charge**, who closes it.

**Mother tongue** and **Other languages spoken** help read the understanding
domain; they are taken over from the previous observation.

Only one observation per resident and grid can be in progress.

:::{tip}
When a resident moves into a sector marked **Adapted Unit** (on admission or
from another wing), Resthome opens an empty observation on its own, with the
reason **Admission or entry into the unit**, unless one is in progress or the
last one is still current. The team fills it during the first month.
:::

## 2. Document the situations

1. Click **Next situation**: the first situation still to document opens.
2. Read the instruction and the caution of the grid.
3. Under **How did the person come through it?**, choose the level.
4. Write the **Observation notes** and, above all, the **Actions or
   alternatives**: what would let the person do it anyway.
5. Click **Save and next**.

Each situation records its own **Observer** and **Observed on** date. The
**Situations** tab lists them all with their level and state (**To document** /
**Documented**); the **Progress** block counts the situations documented and
the alternatives written. **Add a situation** adds one of the resident's own,
beside those of the grid.

## 3. Turn the alternatives into care

From a situation with an alternative:

- **Make it an objective** — opens a care plan objective already filled in (the
  situation, the category of the domain, the notes, the alternative as the
  intervention), on the resident's current care plan, or on a draft plan opened
  for the purpose;
- **Add to the care plan** — adds a care line carrying the alternative.

The objective keeps a link to the situation it comes from. The **Objectives**
and **Care lines** buttons at the top of the observation open what was made of
it. See [Care plans](../soins/plans-de-soins.md).

## 4. Close with the synthesis

Click **Close with its synthesis**. At least one situation must be documented.
The **Synthesis** tab — also posted in the history — sums up each domain, lists
every alternative and what became of it (objective, care line, or *not on the
care plan yet*) and compares the result with the **previous observation** (more
autonomous, less autonomous, unchanged). The **Next observation** is proposed a
year later; set it earlier if needed.

- **Print the grid** prints the observation laid out as the official grid, with
  its synthesis and the comparison.
- **Cancel** stops an observation in progress; what was written stays readable.
- **Reopen** (head nurse) reopens a closed observation. A closed observation is
  part of the clinical record and cannot be deleted.

## The multidisciplinary meeting

Each resident reviewed in the multidisciplinary meeting gets a line
**Capacities (observation grid)**: *never observed*, in progress (with
**Continue**), closed with the next date, or to renew when that date has passed.
**New observation** opens one with the reason *Asked by the multidisciplinary
meeting*.

The meeting's agenda also brings up, on its own:

- an observation whose next date has passed;
- an observation left open for more than 45 days;
- a resident of a sector marked **Adapted Unit** with no observation one month
  after entering it.

## On the care app

When the care app is used, the resident's sheet has a **Capacities** button
(for the roles allowed to read the observations):

- with no observation in progress, it shows the last one and **Start an
  observation** (choose the reason);
- otherwise, the observation in progress domain by domain, with the filter **To
  document** / **All**; tap a situation, choose the level, write the notes and
  the alternative, then **Save**.

What is recorded at the bedside works without a connection and is sent when it
comes back. Closing the observation, with its synthesis, is done at the desk.
