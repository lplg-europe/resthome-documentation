# Quality

:::{rh-description}
The quality tools of Resthome: the complaints register, the residents' council and the observation of the residents' preserved capacities.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Which quality registers does Resthome keep?
: The complaints register, with the day each author was told what came of their complaint, and the residents' council, with each meeting and its written report.

What is the capability observation?
: An observation grid of what a resident can still do in everyday situations. Each alternative written on it becomes an objective or a care line of the care plan.

Are these tools installed everywhere?
: No. The registers are opened from the Serviced Living app, and the capability observation is an optional application an establishment installs when it uses the grid.
:::

and how they were answered, the voice of the people who live there, and the
care built on what each resident can still do.
