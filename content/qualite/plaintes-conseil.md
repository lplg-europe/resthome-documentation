# Complaints register and residents' council

:::{rh-description}
Keep the complaints register and the minutes of the residents' council in WideCare: who complained, what was done, when the author was told, and each council with its written report.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Where are the complaints and the council kept?
: In the Serviced Living app, menu Registers, under Complaints and Council. The residence form also counts its open complaints and its councils.

Can a complaint come from someone who is not a resident?
: Yes. Filed by says who brought it: a resident, a relative, a representative, a visitor or someone else. Resident concerned stays empty when the complaint is about the house.

Why can I not close a complaint?
: A complaint closes only once Author told on is filled: the day the author was told what came of it.

What does a council record hold?
: The date it was held, who was present, the opinions and decisions, and the written report as a file.
:::

Two registers a regulator reads, whatever the country: the **complaints** received
and what came of them, and the meetings of the **council** of the people who
live in the house.

:::{note}
These registers are opened from the **Serviced Living** app, menu **Registers**.
An establishment without that app does not see them.
:::

## The complaints register

Open **Serviced Living → Registers → Complaints**. The list opens on the **Open**
complaints. Click **New** and fill in:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Received on** | The day the complaint was received. |
| **Filed by** | **Resident**, **Family**, **Representative**, **Visitor** or **Other**. |
| **Author** | The name of the person who complained. |
| **Resident concerned** | Empty when the complaint concerns the house. |
| **Residence** | The residence the complaint is about (required in this register). |
| **Subject** | The complaint in a few words. |
| **Complaint** | The complaint in full. |
| **What was done** | The follow-up given. |
| **Author told on** | The day the author was told what came of it. |
| **Attachments** | The letter, email or any document received. |

Each complaint gets a reference when it is saved (for example `CPL-2026-0001`).

### Close a complaint

1. Fill in **What was done** and **Author told on**.
2. Click **Close**: the complaint moves from **Open** to **Closed**.

WideCare refuses to close a complaint without **Author told on**. A closed
complaint can be **reopened**. Each change of status and date is kept in its
history.

## The residents' council

Open **Serviced Living → Registers → Council** and click **New**:

- **Held on** — the date of the meeting;
- **Residence** — where it was held;
- **Present** — the residents and relatives who attended;
- **Opinions and decisions** — what the council said and decided;
- **Written report** — the minutes, as a file.

The list shows each council with its date, residence, attendees and report.

## On the residence

The residence form carries two buttons, **Open complaints** and **Councils**,
which open its registers. When its regime sets a longest gap between two
councils (**Residents' council (months)**), a yellow banner warns when no
council has been held yet or when the last one is too old.
