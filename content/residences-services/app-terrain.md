# The Residence tab of the care app

:::{rh-description}
Work the duty of a serviced residence from the phone: the open calls and the requests to do in the care app's Residence tab, a call written where it is taken, answered and closed on the spot.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Who sees the Residence tab?
: The users who keep the call register of the residence. The tab sits after **Tasks**, with a badge counting the open calls and the requests to do.

What happens without Wi-Fi?
: The gestures are kept on the phone and sent when the connection comes back, each one at the hour it was made, and never twice.

Why is Close the call greyed out?
: When the regime keeps a call register, a call is closed with what came of it: write the follow-up first.
:::

Whoever is on duty in a residence works with the phone in hand. The **Residence**
tab of the [care app](../soins-mobile/index.md) shows what is waiting and records
each gesture in the [call register](appels.md) and the
[service requests](demandes.md).

## On the shift screen

The **My shift** screen shows a **Residence** card: the number of open calls and
requests to do, or *nothing waiting*. Tap it to open the tab.

## Calls

The **Calls** block lists the open calls, with the resident, the unit, the
nature and the hour of the call.

- **Answered** — records the answer now, and you as the person on duty if nobody
  was named.
- **Close** — asks *What came of it?*; write what was done and who was told, then
  tap **Close the call**.

## Take a new call

1. Tap **New call**.
2. Choose **Who is calling?** among the residents of the residence.
3. Choose **What for?** — the nature of the call.
4. Tap **Write it down**.

The call joins the register, open, at the hour it was taken.

## Requests

The **Requests** block lists the requests to do, with what is asked, when, and
who it is assigned to. Tap **Done** once the service is done: it is billed as
from the back office — see [Service requests](demandes.md).
