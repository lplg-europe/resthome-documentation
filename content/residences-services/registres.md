# Complaints and council

:::{rh-description}
Keep the complaints register and the residents' council of a serviced residence: who complained, what was done and when they were told, who sat on the council and its written report.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
When can I close a complaint?
: Once **Author told on** is filled in: a complaint stays open until its author has been told what came of it.

Does the application remind me to hold a council?
: Yes, when the regime sets a rhythm. The residence warns when no council was held yet, or when the last one is older than the regime allows.

Where do I attach the written report of a council?
: On the council, in **Written report**.
:::

Regimes often ask a residence to keep a **register of complaints** and to hold a
**residents' council** at a set rhythm. Both live under **Serviced Living →
Registers**, filed by residence.

## Complaints

In **Serviced Living → Registers → Complaints**, click **New**:

- **Received on**;
- **Filed by** — **Resident**, **Family**, **Representative**, **Visitor** or
  **Other** — and **Author**;
- **Resident concerned** — empty when the complaint concerns the house;
- **Residence**;
- **Subject**, **Complaint** and **What was done**;
- **Attachments**.

When the author has been told what came of it, fill in **Author told on**, then
click **Close**. **Reopen** puts the complaint back to **Open**.

The list opens on the open complaints; the residence's **Open complaints** smart
button opens them for one residence.

## Council

In **Serviced Living → Registers → Council**, record each meeting:

- **Held on** and **Residence**;
- **Present** — the people who attended;
- **Opinions and decisions**;
- **Written report** — the signed report, as a file.

The residence's **Councils** smart button lists its meetings.

:::{admonition} The rhythm of the council
:class: note

When the regime sets **Residents' council (months)** and the residence has
contracts, the residence warns *No council held yet*, or gives the date of the
last council when the next one is overdue.
:::
