# Notice and release of a unit

:::{rh-description}
End a serviced residence contract: give notice with the end date worked out from the regime, then release the unit on the day it is handed back, which stops the billing.
:::

:::{rh-faq}
Does the contract end when the notice runs out?
: No. The notice only sets the date it ends. The unit is billed until you click **Release the unit**, on the day it is actually handed back.

How is the end of the notice worked out?
: From the regime: the notice of the resident or of the manager, in months or in days, or the shorter notice of the trial period while it runs. You can change the date when the parties agreed otherwise.

Can I release a unit in advance?
: No. A unit is released on the day it is handed back: a date still to come is refused.

What does "To release" mean?
: Nobody lives in the unit any more — every occupant has left — but the contract still runs, so the unit is still billed. Release it once the keys are back.

A resident wants to move to another flat of the residence. How?
: A contract holds one unit. Release the current one, sign a contract on the other unit, then move in.
:::

A contract ends in two moves: the **notice**, which sets an end date, and the
**release**, which records the day the unit is handed back. Billing stops at the
release, never before.

## 1. Give notice

On a **Running** contract, click **Give notice** and fill in the window:

- **Given by** — **The resident** or **The manager**;
- **Notice given on** — today by default;
- **Ends on** — worked out from the regime, editable if the parties agreed
  otherwise;
- **Reason** — required for a notice from the manager when the regime asks
  the manager to state it.

Click **Give notice**. The contract becomes **Under notice**, and a **Notice**
tab keeps who gave it, when, why and when it ends.

## 2. Release the unit

On the day the unit is handed back, click **Release the unit** — on a **Running**
or an **Under notice** contract.

- **Released on** — the day the unit was handed back, today by default. Billing
  stops here.
- **Why** — the reason of the departure, asked when occupants still live in the
  unit (for example **Return to family / home** or **Deceased**).

Click **Release the unit**. The contract becomes **Ended**, the occupants leave
on that date, and **Unit released on** shows on the contract. The billing of the
months already invoiced is corrected by itself at the next generation — see
[Billing a residence](facturation.md).

:::{admonition} Released before the end of the notice
:class: warning

When the notice is still running, the window says so: *The notice is still
running: billing stops on this day all the same.* Release on the real day the
unit is handed back, then settle the rest of the notice with the resident if
the contract says so.
:::

## When the unit is "to release"

When every occupant has left — a move to a nursing home, for example — the
contract keeps running and shows the **To release** ribbon. The unit is still
billed: release it as soon as the keys are back.

The **To release** filter of **Serviced Living → Contracts** lists these
contracts, and the billing dashboard counts them.

## After a death

The regime's **Days due after a death** caps what is billed after the death of
the last occupant. At 0, the unit is billed until it is released.

## Cancelling a contract

**Cancel** only exists on a contract **To sign** or **Signed**: a contract that
has started is ended by releasing the unit, never cancelled.
